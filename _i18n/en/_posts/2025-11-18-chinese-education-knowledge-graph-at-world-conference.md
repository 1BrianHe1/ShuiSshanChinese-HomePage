On November 18th, at the **2025 World Chinese Conference**, the Shuishan Chinese team, as the technical support team, showcased the **International Chinese Education Knowledge Graph** to Chinese education experts from around the world. The project received high attention from participating experts and was featured in a special report by **CCTV's "Morning News"** program.

### 🏛️ Official Support

The **International Chinese Education Knowledge Graph** project is supported by the **Center for Language Education and Cooperation under the Ministry of Education**. It is a large-scale semantic network systematically constructed by the "Shuishan Chinese" team based on three years of intensive accumulation.

### 📊 Three Major Thematic Resources

The knowledge graph integrates three core thematic resources:

#### 1️⃣ Language Knowledge Layer
- Systematically organizes fundamental language elements such as Chinese characters, vocabulary, and grammar
- Constructs a complete language knowledge network system
- Supports standardization and systematization of language teaching

#### 2️⃣ Assessment Resource Layer
- Deeply integrates the "International Chinese Language Education Chinese Proficiency Standards"
- Incorporates the "HSK Outline" and "HSK 3.0 Examination Outline"
- Transforms knowledge points into quantifiable test resources
- Achieves integrated "teaching, learning, and assessment"

#### 3️⃣ Cultural Extension Layer
- Organically links language and cultural content
- Integrates idioms, allusions, and cultural background knowledge
- Embodies the "language + culture" dual-track teaching philosophy

### 📺 CCTV Coverage

CCTV's "Morning News" program featured a special report on the knowledge graph project, showcasing to national audiences:
- The innovative value and technical features of the knowledge graph
- Its application prospects in international Chinese education
- The R&D capabilities and social contributions of the "Shuishan Chinese" team

### 🌟 Technical Highlights

The knowledge graph features the following distinctive characteristics:
- **Large-scale**: Covering over 380,000 language nodes and 1 million semantic relationships
- **Multi-dimensional**: Integrating 16 types of entities to construct a multi-dimensional knowledge network
- **Standardized**: Strictly adhering to the international Chinese education standard system
- **Practical**: Supporting the entire process of teaching, learning, and assessment

### 🎯 Social Impact

As the first large-scale international Chinese education knowledge graph, this project not only provides important technical support for Chinese language teaching but also offers an innovative paradigm for promoting the digital and intelligent transformation of international Chinese education, receiving widespread recognition from academia and industry.
