On October 4th, at the **23rd New York International Chinese Language Teaching Symposium**, our **Professor Qian Weining** and **Lecturer Wang Chunyang** were invited to present academic reports, sharing research achievements and practical experiences of the "Shuishan Chinese" platform in intelligent Chinese language teaching.

### 📢 Presentation Content

The two scholars engaged in in-depth discussions on **applications of intelligent technology in international Chinese education**, introducing the core technical architecture of the "Shuishan Chinese" platform, innovative teaching models, and application effectiveness in actual teaching scenarios.

### 🌟 Key Topics

- **Technology Empowering Teaching**: How to utilize AI technology to enhance Chinese language teaching quality
- **Personalized Learning**: Data-driven personalized learning path design
- **Teaching Model Innovation**: Exploration and practice of scenario-based and gamified learning models
- **Platform Application Cases**: Sharing successful experiences from domestic and international teaching applications

### 🌐 International Impact

The New York International Chinese Language Teaching Symposium is an important academic event in the field of international Chinese education. This presentation showcased the "Shuishan Chinese" team's cutting-edge exploration in **Language Education Technology**, receiving high attention and positive response from participating experts and scholars.
