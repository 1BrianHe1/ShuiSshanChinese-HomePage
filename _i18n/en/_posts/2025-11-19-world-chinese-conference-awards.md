At the **2025 World Chinese Conference** held in Beijing in November, "Shuishan Chinese" received multiple prestigious awards for its synergistic innovation in technology and teaching methodology.

### 🥇 Gold Teaching Tool

**Shuishan Online** was selected as **"2025 Global Chinese Alliance Digital Teaching Gold Teaching Tool"**, which fully recognizes the platform's technological innovation and practical effectiveness in intelligent teaching.

### 🤝 Gold Partner

The team was also among the first to join the new Chinese Alliance platform as **"2025 Global Chinese Alliance Digital Teaching Gold Partner"**, becoming an officially recommended core technology product. This marks the official recognition of "Shuishan Chinese" in the digital transformation of international Chinese education.

### 🌟 Far-reaching Significance

These honors not only affirm the team's existing work but also provide important opportunities for the future development of "Shuishan Chinese", while setting higher requirements for advancing synergistic innovation between digital technology and teaching models.

The team will continue to uphold the philosophy of "technology empowering education" and provide higher quality intelligent learning and teaching services for Chinese learners and educators worldwide.
