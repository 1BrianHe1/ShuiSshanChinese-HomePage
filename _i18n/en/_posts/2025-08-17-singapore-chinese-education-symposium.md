On August 17th, at the **Singapore International Chinese Education Symposium**, our **Professor Qian Weining**, **Professor Dong Qiwen**, and **Professor Pu Peng** were invited to present academic reports, introducing the latest research progress and practical applications of the "Shuishan Chinese" platform to participating experts and scholars.

### 📊 Presentation Themes

The three professors showcased the innovative features of "Shuishan Chinese" from different perspectives:
- **Technical Architecture and System Design**: Introducing the platform's overall technical solution
- **Knowledge Graph Construction**: Sharing research achievements in international Chinese education knowledge graphs
- **Intelligent Teaching Applications**: Demonstrating practical applications of AI technology in teaching scenarios

### 🤝 Regional Cooperation

The presentation specifically introduced the application and promotion of "Shuishan Chinese" in **Singapore and Southeast Asia**, including:
- Localized textbook adaptation and resource distribution
- Multilingual learning support
- Deep integration of regional teaching characteristics with platform functions

### 🎯 Academic Value

The Singapore International Chinese Education Symposium gathered Chinese education experts from Southeast Asia and around the world. This presentation not only demonstrated the platform's technical strength but also laid a solid foundation for promoting the application of "Shuishan Chinese" in the Southeast Asian region.
