On June 26th, at the workshop held by the **Turin Confucius Institute in Italy**, our **Professor Qian Weining** and **Associate Professor Lan Yunshi** were invited to present academic reports, engaging in in-depth exchanges with teachers, students, and local Chinese education practitioners at the Turin Confucius Institute.

### 🇮🇹 Deepening Cooperation

This workshop was an important opportunity for "Shuishan Chinese" to establish **regional node cooperation** with the **Turin Confucius Institute**. The two scholars provided detailed introductions on:
- **Platform Functions and Features**: Showcasing core teaching tools of "Shuishan Chinese"
- **Application Promotion Plan**: Discussing localized applications of the platform in Europe
- **Innovative Cooperation Models**: Co-building digital teaching demonstration nodes

### 🎓 Demonstration and Leadership

The Turin Confucius Institute, as an important overseas partner of "Shuishan Chinese", has conducted highly effective digital teaching innovation practices using the platform. This cooperation project was subsequently successfully selected as a **"2025 Confucius Institute Digital Application and Practice Demonstration Case"** at the 2025 World Chinese Conference.

### 🌍 European Strategy

This workshop marked significant progress in the application and promotion of "Shuishan Chinese" in Europe, providing valuable experience for subsequent implementation in more Confucius Institutes across European countries and regions.

### 🤝 Mutual Learning

During the workshop, both parties engaged in in-depth discussions on topics such as **synergistic innovation of intelligent technology and Chinese teaching**, **localized resource development**, and **teacher training and capability enhancement**, reaching multiple cooperation consensus.
