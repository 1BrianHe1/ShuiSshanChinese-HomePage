At the **26th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED 2025)**, our **Associate Professor Lan Yunshi** delivered an outstanding tutorial in collaboration with renowned international scholars.

### 🌟 International Collaboration

This tutorial was jointly presented by Associate Professor Lan Yunshi with the following international scholars:
- Scholars from **University of Sheffield, UK**
- Experts from **British Council**
- Researchers from **Hosei University, Japan**

### 📊 Tutorial Theme

**"NLP and Generative AI for Language Learning and Assessment: Synergies between Research and Practice"**

Based on a systematic review of **AI technologies in language learning and assessment**, the tutorial explored in depth:
- Applications of Natural Language Processing (NLP) in language learning
- Practice of Generative AI technologies in language assessment
- Paths for synergistic innovation between research and practice
- Challenges and opportunities in technology implementation

### 🔬 Significance and Impact

AIED (Artificial Intelligence in Education) is a top-tier international conference in the field of AI in education. This tutorial showcased the "Shuishan Chinese" team's cutting-edge research achievements in **Language Education Technology (LET)**, providing important theoretical support and practical guidance for promoting the application of AI technology in international Chinese education.
