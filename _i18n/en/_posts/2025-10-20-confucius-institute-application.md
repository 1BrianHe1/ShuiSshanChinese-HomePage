In 2025, "Shuishan Chinese" moved from the laboratory to real teaching scenarios, gradually expanding from campus to Confucius Institutes worldwide, building a replicable paradigm of **"intelligent teaching, standardized management, and personalized services"**.

### 🇮🇹 Turin Confucius Institute: Regional Node Demonstration

The team collaborated with the **Turin Confucius Institute** to establish a "regional node" and conduct digital teaching innovation using the platform. This project was successfully selected as a **"2025 Confucius Institute Digital Application and Practice Demonstration Case"** at the 2025 World Chinese Conference.

### 🌐 Multi-country Promotion: Localized Distribution

In the **United States, Singapore**, and other regions, the team utilized the platform's **localized textbook embedding** and **multilingual adaptation** functions to achieve "localized" distribution and management of educational resources:

- **Centralized Development**: Unified resource development and technical maintenance by the team
- **Distributed Application**: Overseas partner institutions as distribution nodes for localized application
- **Full-cycle Loop**: Connecting resource production, classroom organization, and educational administration

### 🏫 Campus Practice: Deep Integration

The platform is deeply integrated into the teaching practice of **East China Normal University's School of International Chinese Language and Culture**, utilizing the "Shuishan Online" technical foundation to achieve efficient empowerment of language teaching and support the cultivation of **"Chinese teachers in digital transformation"** as composite talents.

### 🚀 Future Outlook

"Shuishan Chinese" will continue to uphold the philosophy that "the value of technology lies in application" and promote platform implementation in Confucius Institutes in more countries and regions, contributing to the digital transformation of global Chinese education.
