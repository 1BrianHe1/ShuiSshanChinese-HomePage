In December, at the **2nd China Data Conference** held in Guiyang, our **Professor Qian Weining** organized the **"Data-Driven Computational Pedagogy"** forum, promoting academic exchange and industry cooperation in educational digital transformation.

### 🎯 Forum Theme

The forum focused on **data-driven computational pedagogy**, exploring how to use big data technology and artificial intelligence methods to promote educational model innovation, attracting many experts and scholars from academia and industry to participate in exchanges.

### 📢 Project Report

At the forum, our **Professor Gao Ming** introduced the latest progress of the **"Shuishan Chinese" platform** and reported in detail on the progress of the **National Natural Science Foundation Key Project**:

- **Platform Architecture**: Showcased the technical architecture and innovative features of "Shuishan Chinese"
- **Application Effectiveness**: Shared practical achievements of the platform on and off campus
- **Project Progress**: Reported phased research results of the national key project
- **Future Planning**: Discussed the development direction and technical roadmap of the platform

### 🌟 Continuous Output

In addition, multiple members of the R&D team have shared the team's research work and achievements in **Language Education Technology (LET)** at academic conferences and forums in the fields of **computer science** and **international Chinese education** over the past year, promoting deep integration between academia and industry.

### 🔬 Research Directions

The team continues to conduct in-depth research in the following directions:
- Data-driven personalized learning path planning
- Intelligent teaching content generation and recommendation
- Multimodal learning behavior analysis and assessment
- Application of knowledge graphs in language education
