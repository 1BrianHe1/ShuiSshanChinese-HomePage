At the **International Chinese Teachers' Digital Literacy and Capability Enhancement Forum** of the **2025 World Chinese Conference**, our **Professor Qian Weining** delivered a keynote speech titled **"Digital Literacy Enhancement of International Chinese Teachers in the Digital Intelligence Era"**, which sparked enthusiastic discussions among participating experts and scholars.

### 📊 Presentation Content

Professor Qian Weining systematically shared valuable experience from the R&D and application promotion process of "Shuishan Chinese":

#### 🔬 Synergistic Innovation of Technology and Education

Introduced observations and reflections on **synergistic innovation between technology and teaching models** during the platform's R&D and promotion:
- How technology truly empowers teaching
- How teaching needs drive technological innovation
- Key elements of integrating technology and education
- Sustainable development paths for innovative models

#### 🏗️ Platform Construction Logic

In-depth analysis of the **internal logic of learning platform construction**:
- **Demand-oriented**: Starting from real teaching scenarios
- **Technical Support**: Deep integration of AI technology and educational philosophy
- **Model Innovation**: Personalized, scenario-based, data-driven learning models
- **Ecosystem Construction**: Full-cycle loop of teaching, learning, practicing, testing, and managing

### 🎯 Core Viewpoints

The presentation emphasized that in the digital intelligence era, enhancing international Chinese teachers' digital literacy is not only about mastering technical tools, but more importantly:
- Understanding the educational philosophy behind technology
- Organically integrating technology into instructional design
- Using data to gain insights into learning patterns
- Promoting innovative transformation of teaching models

### 🌟 Enthusiastic Response

The presentation received high praise from participating experts and scholars, providing important practical reference and theoretical guidance for teacher training and digital transformation in the field of international Chinese education.
